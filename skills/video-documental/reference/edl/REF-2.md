## 5 · What makes it fire — and what a first draft lacks (ranked)
First draft checked at 2fps: 13 beats in 19.4s, one change every ~1.5s; stills and pages; one camera take; one
NASA image ×3; captions white throughout; strobe of OpenAI page crops; music laid under, ticks on pops.

1. **Moving footage, and lots of it.** REF-2 is 62% moving clips (films, TV, concerts, news, stock, archival) — ~200 sequential pictures
   in 94s. The draft is stills + screenshots + the speaker's face; nothing in it moves except our tweens. → Source **video** first (yt-dlp clip
   sections, Pexels/Pixabay/archive.org/NASA video), stills second.
2. **Metaphor, not just evidence.** 59% of REF-2's picture time is a metaphor for the word (a fighter plane for "try", a horse with a
   flag for "the best", Michael Jackson for "the real deal", Breaking Bad for "win or die", a cheetah for "no plan B"). The draft only
   showed the literal thing (OpenAI building, posts, pages). → For every line pick **one literal + one metaphor**; for abstract
   words, a famous film/TV/celebrity moment.
3. **The pace inside a line.** REF-2 changes picture on nearly every word in a fast phrase (MJ: 10 shots in 3.8s), and lands
   one-word strobes of 2–4f. The draft held 1.3–4.3s per beat. → Target a picture every **0.4–0.8s** in the cold open, strobes on
   single words.
4. **Strobes that are shape-matched.** Every REF-2 strobe is a family of the same shape (circles, spirals) under one overlay, with a tick
   per image. Ours was random page crops. → Build `strobe` with a shape theme + a fixed overlay + hopping square.
5. **Sound design that is a score.** REF-2's music changes section on every chapter card, drops to silence before the ident and under
   the punchline, has click trains per pop, zaps on flips, a riser into a glitch. The draft had one bed + generic ticks/hits. → Pick a
   bed with sections (or cut two), cut it on the chapter cards, **mute it for the punchline**, add risers/zaps/clicks per device.
   (The reference palette is clicks, zaps, risers, sub booms — `sfx/ev1kit/` synthesises those classes.)
6. **Transitions with recipes.** White-block shatter, whip-carry smear, double negatives into chapters, colour-flash frames, sepia film
   burn, censor bars, orange fire smears, light-leak bars, zoom-punch, zoom-tunnel. The draft had tile reveals, one datamosh and one
   white flash. → One named bridge on every ground change.
7. **Chapter cards are events.** Each has its own recipe (fly-in card → painting with the title behind the subject; typed title over a
   push; posterise → LED → starburst → zoom-through), entered by a double negative, with the music changing under it. The draft had no
   chapter moment.
8. **Captions that change character.** Italic serif on the stressed phrase, 2× bold for key words, black on light pages, word cards on
   empty white/black with a block cursor, a subliminal logo. Ours were white sans everywhere.
9. **Rebuilt UIs that perform.** Chesky's profile tilted in 3D over a posterised skyline with the bio typing; a sentence typing across
   5 platforms; a wordmark backspacing into its logo. Our terminal scroll is the right idea but static and flat (no 3D tilt, no
   posterised world behind it, no cursor typing on the key line).
10. **Colour islands.** Mostly grey with saturated bursts (blue bacteria, red virus, orange skyline, yellow flash). The draft kept one
    teal look per beat and the face in natural colour throughout.
